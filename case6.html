The Cartographer's Mark

A True-Crime Murder — The Killer Left a Signature
Difficulty
Hard

The Situation

Dana Rowe built one of the most listened-to true-crime podcasts in the country — fifty million downloads, a Webby, and a reputation for naming names that lawyers told her not to name. Last Tuesday night she was found dead in her home studio. Single blunt-force blow. No signs of a break-in.

Time of death: Estimated 10:30 PM – 11:15 PM, Tuesday.
Location: Her home recording studio, Clapham.
Discovered by: Her producer, arriving Wednesday morning.
The mark: A red handprint, pressed onto the wall above her body — deliberate, careful, dry by the time she was found.
⚠ The red handprint is not what it appears. The "Cartographer" killing it references was never solved and has three known imitators. Do not let the signature pull you toward the wrong answer.

Dana had been two weeks from publishing a final episode — one that would name the person behind a cold case she'd spent eighteen months investigating. Five people knew how close she was. One of them wasn't willing to wait and find out what she'd found.

This case has three threads. Each one matters. One of them leads almost all the way to the door — and stops short. The real answer requires you to check the same alibi from two different directions.
