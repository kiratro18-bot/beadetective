Difficulty

The Situation

Roland Ashby — retired High Court judge, 67, recently widowed — was found dead in his study at Westhaven Manor at 11:20 PM on Friday. A single blow to the back of the head. His safe was open. His papers were disturbed. And a bundle of letters that were there that morning were gone by midnight.

Time of death: Estimated 10:30 PM – 11:10 PM, Friday.
Location: The study, Westhaven Manor, Barrowfield village.
Discovered by: The housekeeper, returning from the garden.
What's missing: A bundle of personal correspondence from the safe — contents unknown to anyone but Roland.

Seven people were within reach of Westhaven that night. Three were inside the property. Four were in the village or on the approach road. Statements have been taken. They don't all agree.

Use the location map to filter suspects by where they claim to have been. The map will help — but remember that where someone says they were and where they were are not the same thing.

There are three threads to follow. One of them leads almost to the study door — and stops just short. The real answer requires you to check one alibi from two separate directions.
