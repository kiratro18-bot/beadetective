Difficulty

The Situation

The Aldgate University Special Collections vault holds one of the most valuable objects ever to pass through this city — a 1623 First Folio of Shakespeare, on loan from the Harwick estate for a three-month exhibition. Insured for £8 million. Access restricted to key-card holders only, with a digital log of every entry.

What's missing: The Aldgate Folio — one of only 235 known surviving copies of the First Folio.
Window: Saturday 6:00 PM (last confirmed sighting at vault check) to Sunday 8:00 AM (discovered missing on opening).
Entry log: No key-card entries after 5:58 PM Saturday. Either the log was bypassed — or someone on the inside knew how.

Five people had the means, the access, or a reason. The truth is in the details, not the motives — more than one person here wanted that book gone. What you're looking for is the one whose story doesn't hold together once you push on it from two directions at once.

This one won't give itself away on the first question. Take your time with each person. Some threads only connect once you've pulled on both ends.
